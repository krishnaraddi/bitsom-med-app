# HealGoa-AI
## AI-Powered Integrated Medical Tourism & Wellness Ecosystem

### Transforming Goa into the World's First AI-Enabled Healthcare & Wellness Destination

---

## Overview

HealGoa-AI is a Vertical Agentic AI Platform designed to revolutionize medical tourism by integrating modern healthcare, AYUSH therapies, wellness tourism, travel services, insurance, and post-treatment care into a single intelligent ecosystem.

Aligned with India's **Heal in India** initiative, HealGoa-AI positions Goa as a global destination for healthcare, wellness, recovery, and preventive medicine.

---

## Problem Statement

Medical tourism currently suffers from fragmented experiences across multiple stakeholders:

- Hospitals
- Doctors
- AYUSH Centers
- Wellness Resorts
- Insurance Providers
- Travel Operators
- Accommodation Providers

Patients face challenges in:

- Finding the right treatment
- Comparing healthcare providers
- Coordinating appointments
- Managing travel logistics
- Accessing recovery and wellness programs
- Receiving continuous follow-up care

---

## Multi-Agent Architecture

### Patient Concierge Agent
Guides patients from inquiry to treatment.

### Clinical Decision Support Agent
Analyzes patient data and assists treatment planning.

### Medical Imaging Agent
Supports analysis of X-rays, MRIs, and CT scans.

### AYUSH Recommendation Agent
Suggests integrated Ayurveda, Yoga, Siddha, Unani, and Homeopathy pathways.

### Hospital Matching Agent
Recommends the best hospitals, specialists, and treatment packages.

### Medical Tourism Coordinator Agent
Handles visas, travel, accommodation, and logistics.

### Appointment Scheduling Agent
Coordinates consultations, diagnostics, and treatments.

### Remote Monitoring Agent
Tracks recovery through wearables and IoT devices.

### Insurance & Claims Agent
Facilitates coverage validation and approvals.

### Wellness Recovery Agent
Creates personalized rehabilitation and wellness programs.
